[Safaricom PLC] is Kenya's largest telecommunications provider, serving 37.1 million active customers. It is the home of M-PESA, the world's first and most successful mobile money transfer system.

Ranked Kenya's most trusted brand with a 87.2% trust score, Safaricom has revolutionized how Kenyans communicate, transact, and access financial services.

Key services include:

  • Mobile: Voice, SMS, and data services across 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G networks
  • M-PESA: Mobile money transfers, payments, savings, and loans
  • Enterprise: Business solutions, IoT, and cloud services
  • Digital: M-PESA App, Safaricom App, and Blaze by Safaricom
