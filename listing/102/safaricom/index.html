How to Register for M-PESA

1. Dial *234# from your Safaricom line
2. Select "M-PESA" then "Register"
3. Enter your ID number and name as shown on ID
4. Create a 4-digit PIN – keep it secure
5. Visit an M-PESA agent for full activation (if required)
Free registration – no charges apply

How to Reset M-PESA PIN

1. Dial *234# and select "M-PESA"
2. Select "Forgot PIN" or "Reset PIN"
3. Enter your ID number for verification
4. Create a new 4-digit PIN
5. Confirm your new PIN to complete
Keep your PIN secure – never share it
📤

How to Send Money via M-PESA

1. Dial *234# or open the M-PESA App
2. Select "Send Money"
3. Enter the recipient's phone number
4. Enter the amount you wish to send
5. Enter your PIN to confirm the transaction
Instant delivery – recipient receives SMS confirmation
🔄

How to Reverse M-PESA Transaction

1. Call Safaricom Customer Care on 100 (24/7)
2. Provide the transaction reference number (from SMS)
3. Confirm the recipient's number and amount
4. Request transaction reversal (if eligible)
5. Wait for confirmation SMS of reversal
Reversal only applies if funds have not been withdrawn

❓ M-PESA FAQs – Your Questions Answered

Q:
How do I register for M-PESA online?

Dial *234# from your Safaricom line, select "M-PESA" then "Register." Follow the prompts to enter your ID and create a PIN.

Q:
How to check my M-PESA Fuliza limit?

Dial *234#, select "Fuliza," then choose "Check Fuliza limit." Alternatively, check via the M-PESA App.

Q:
How to reverse an M-PESA transaction sent to the wrong number?

Call Safaricom Customer Care on 100 (24/7) with your transaction reference number. They will assist with the reversal if eligible.

Q:
How to reset M-PESA PIN if I have forgotten it?

Dial *234#, select "M-PESA" then "Forgot PIN." Enter your ID number and create a new PIN.

Q:
What is the daily limit for sending money via M-PESA?

The daily sending limit is KSh 35,000 for standard accounts. Fuliza limits vary based on your usage and repayment history.

Q:
How to pay bills using M-PESA?

Dial *234#, select "Pay Bills," choose the biller (e.g., KPLC, water, school fees), enter the account number and amount, then confirm with your PIN.

💰 Category 1: M-PESA & Mobile Money

Q1:

How do I register for M-PESA online using my Safaricom number?

Answer: To register for M-PESA, dial *234# from your Safaricom line, select "M-PESA" then "Register." You'll need your ID number and full name as shown on your ID. Follow the prompts to create a 4-digit PIN. Once registered, you can start sending money, paying bills, checking your Fuliza limit, and accessing M-PESA loans and savings. For assisted registration, visit any M-PESA agent or Safaricom Shop with your ID.

📝 Dial *234# 🆔 ID required 🔑 4-digit PIN
Q2:

How to check my M-PESA Fuliza limit and outstanding balance?

Answer: To check your Fuliza limit, dial *234# and select "Fuliza" then "Check Fuliza limit". You'll see your available limit and outstanding balance. Alternatively, use the M-PESA App – tap "Fuliza" and view your limit and balance. How to apply for Fuliza: You're automatically enrolled when you use M-PESA and your balance is insufficient. How to increase Fuliza limit: Regular use and timely repayment increases your limit. How to repay Fuliza: Repayments are automatically deducted from your M-PESA balance when you receive money.

💰 *234# Fuliza 📱 M-PESA App 🔄 Auto-repayment
Q3:

How to reverse an M-PESA transaction sent to the wrong number?

Answer: If you sent money to the wrong number, call Safaricom Customer Care on 100 (from your Safaricom line) or +254 722 000 000 (from any network) immediately. You'll need: (1) The transaction reference number (from the confirmation SMS), (2) The recipient's phone number, (3) The amount sent. The customer care team will investigate and request a reversal if the funds have not been withdrawn. Note: Reversals are only possible if the recipient hasn't withdrawn the money. How to avoid: Always double‑check the recipient's number before confirming.

📞 Call 100 🔄 Transaction reference ⏱️ Act quickly

How to activate Safaricom eSIM and transfer it to a new phone?
