Absa Bank Kenya is a cornerstone of Kenya's financial sector, with a rich heritage dating back to 1916. This transformation marked a new era of African leadership, regional integration, and a renewed commitment to empowering Kenyans with innovative financial solutions.

With over 100 years of banking experience in Kenya, Absa Bank has played a pivotal role in the country's economic development. The bank has supported generations of Kenyans – from individuals and families to small businesses and large corporations – helping them achieve their financial goals and build a better future. Today, Absa Bank Kenya continues to innovate and evolve, leveraging the strength and resources of the Absa Group to deliver world-class banking services to millions of customers across Kenya.

Absa Bank Kenya's product portfolio is designed to meet the diverse financial needs of Kenyan customers. The bank offers a comprehensive range of personal banking services including savings accounts, current accounts, loans, and credit cards; business banking solutions such as corporate banking, trade finance, and treasury services; wealth management including investments, insurance, and financial planning; and digital banking through the Absa Mobile App, online banking, and payment solutions.

As part of the Absa Group, Absa Bank Kenya benefits from the financial strength, expertise, and scale of one of Africa's largest financial services organizations. The Absa Group operates in 12 African countries, serving millions of customers with a comprehensive range of banking, insurance, wealth management, and investment solutions. This pan-African presence gives Absa Bank Kenya a unique perspective on the opportunities and challenges facing the continent, enabling the bank to deliver insightful, relevant, and innovative financial solutions to Kenyan customers.

1970s

Expansion & Growth

Barclays Bank expanded its branch network across Kenya, becoming a household name and a trusted financial partner for millions of Kenyans.

1990s

Digital Transformation

Barclays Bank embraced digital innovation, introducing online banking, ATM networks, and mobile banking to customers across Kenya.

2018

Transition to Absa Bank Kenya

Barclays Bank Kenya rebranded to Absa Bank Kenya as part of the separation from Barclays PLC and the formation of the Absa Group – a proudly African financial services organization.

2024

Innovation & Growth

Absa Bank Kenya continues to innovate and grow, leveraging the strength of the Absa Group to deliver world-class banking services to millions of Kenyans across the country.
