How to File a Claim with APEX Insurance

01

Step 1: Report Your Claim

Contact APEX Insurance immediately to report your claim. You can reach us through multiple channels:

  • Phone: Call our 24/7 claims hotline at +254 711 555 777
  • WhatsApp: Send a message to +254 711 555 777 for instant claims assistance
  • Online: Submit your claim online through our customer portal
  • Email: Send claim details to claims@apexinsurance.co.ke
  • In-Person: Visit any APEX Insurance branch across Kenya

Tip: Report your claim as soon as possible after the incident to ensure fast processing.

02

Step 2: Provide Claim Documentation

Our claims team will guide you through the documentation requirements for your specific insurance claim. Common documents include:

  • Completed claim form – Available online or at any branch
  • Police report – For motor insurance claims or theft claims
  • Medical reports – For personal accident insurance claims
  • Photos/Video evidence – Documenting damage or loss
  • Witness statements – Supporting third-party claims
  • Policy documents – Your APEX Insurance policy number

Digital Advantage: Upload all documents through our online claims portal for faster processing.

03

Step 3: Claims Assessment

Once we receive your claim documents, our experienced claims assessors will review your case thoroughly:

  • Validation: Verify policy coverage and claim eligibility
  • Investigation: Review incident details and supporting evidence
  • Assessment: Evaluate damage or loss to determine claim value
  • Approval: Make a fair claims decision based on policy terms

Timeline: Most claims are assessed within 24 hours of receiving complete documentation.

04

Step 4: Claims Payment

Once your claim is approved, we process payment quickly and efficiently:

  • Direct Bank Transfer: Payment to your bank account within 48 hours
  • M-Pesa Payment: Instant mobile money payment for approved claims
  • Check Payment: Issued at any APEX Insurance branch
  • Repair Authorization: For motor insurance claims, we can authorize approved repair shops

Guarantee: We aim to settle 95% of claims within 48 hours of claim approval.

05

Step 5: Claims Follow-Up

Our commitment to customer satisfaction continues after your claim is settled:

  • Customer Feedback: We'll reach out to gather your claims experience
  • Policy Review: Opportunity to review and update your insurance coverage
  • Claims Assistance: Ongoing support for any post-claim questions
  • Loyalty Rewards: No-claim bonuses for safe policyholders

We Care: Your satisfaction is our priority. We're here to support you through every step of your insurance journey.
