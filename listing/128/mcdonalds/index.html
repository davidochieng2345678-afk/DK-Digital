Brand Story

The McDonald's Story: From a Small Drive-In to Global Icon

Discover the remarkable journey of how two brothers and a visionary businessman built the world's largest fast food empire.

The Birth of a Legend: San Bernardino (1940)

McDonald's story begins in 1940 when brothers Richard "Dick" McDonald and Maurice "Mac" McDonald opened a barbeque restaurant on Route 66 in San Bernardino, California. The restaurant was a typical drive-in of the era, featuring carhops and a menu of barbeque items. However, the brothers noticed that 80% of their revenue came from hamburger sales, so they decided to pivot.

In 1948, the McDonald brothers introduced a revolutionary concept: a self-service drive-in restaurant with a simplified menu of just 9 items — hamburgers, cheeseburgers, soft drinks, and fries. They implemented the "Speedee Service System," an assembly-line approach to food preparation that reduced wait times and ensured consistency. This system became the blueprint for modern fast food and laid the foundation for McDonald's global success.

Founded 1940 Speedee Service System San Bernardino, CA

Ray Kroc: The Visionary Who Built an Empire

In 1954, a milkshake machine salesman named Ray Kroc stumbled upon McDonald's. He was amazed by the efficiency of the Speedee Service System and saw unlimited potential for expansion. Kroc convinced the McDonald brothers to let him franchise the concept, and in 1955, he opened the first McDonald's franchise in Des Plaines, Illinois — the first of thousands.

Kroc's vision was bold and relentless. He focused on standardization, quality control, and real estate, creating a franchise model that would dominate the industry. In 1961, Kroc purchased the company from the McDonald brothers for $2.7 million — a sum that would prove to be one of the greatest business investments in history. Under Kroc's leadership, McDonald's expanded globally, becoming a symbol of American culture and capitalism.

1955: First Franchise $2.7M Purchase (1961) Global Expansion

The Golden Arches: A Symbol of American Culture

The golden arches are one of the most recognized symbols in the world. Originally designed by Stanley Clark Meston in 1952, the two yellow arches were meant to draw attention to the restaurant from the freeway. The design was simple, bold, and instantly memorable — qualities that would define the McDonald's brand.

In 1968, the arches were incorporated into the company logo, creating the now-iconic "M" that has become synonymous with fast food. The golden arches represent quality, consistency, and the American dream — a beacon of hope for franchisees and a source of comfort for customers around the world.

1952: Arches Designed 1968: Logo Created Global Icon

The Big Mac: The Burger That Changed the World

In 1968, McDonald's introduced the Big Mac — a double-patty burger with special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, and onions on a sesame seed bun. Created by Jim Delligatti, a franchisee in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the Big Mac was designed to compete with Burger King's Whopper and quickly became a customer favorite.

The Big Mac's special sauce — a secret recipe of mayonnaise, relish, and spices — became legendary. The burger's "two all-beef patties, special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, onions on a sesame seed bun" jingle became one of the most memorable advertising campaigns in history. Today, over 550 million Big Macs are sold each year, making it one of the most successful food products of all time.

1968: Introduced 550M+ Sold Annually Global Icon

Chicken McNuggets & Menu Innovation

In 1983, McDonald's introduced Chicken McNuggets — tender, juicy pieces of chicken breast meat in a crispy coating. The product was an instant success, revolutionizing the fast food chicken category and becoming one of McDonald's best-selling menu items. Today, over 2.5 billion Chicken McNuggets are sold globally each year.

McDonald's has continued to innovate its menu over the decades. The brand introduced the Egg McMuffin in 1971 (creating the breakfast fast food category), the McFlurry in 1995, and the McCafé coffee line in 2009. The brand has also adapted to changing consumer preferences with healthier options like salads, wraps, and fruit, and has embraced sustainability with sustainable sourcing and packaging.

1983: McNuggets 2009: McCafé Sustainability Focus

McDonald's has a growing presence in Kenya and Africa, with locations in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Eldoret. The brand has adapted to local tastes and preferences, offering specialized menu items while maintaining its global standards of quality, service, and cleanliness.

McDonald's growing presence in Kenya reflects the country's economic growth and increasing appetite for fast food.

Core Values: Quality, Service, Cleanliness, Value

McDonald's is built on four core values — Quality, Service, Cleanliness, and Value (QSCV). These principles, introduced by Ray Kroc, have been the foundation of the brand's success for over 70 years:

  • Quality: Using the finest ingredients and preparing food with care and precision
  • Service: Providing fast, friendly, and efficient service to every customer
  • Cleanliness: Maintaining spotless restaurants, kitchens, and restrooms
  • Value: Offering affordable, high-quality food that satisfies hunger

These values have been instilled in every employee and franchisee, creating a culture of excellence that has driven McDonald's success worldwide. The brand's commitment to these values has earned it customer loyalty and trust across generations.

Q

What is McDonald's known for?

McDonald's is known for its iconic golden arches, the Big Mac burger, Chicken McNuggets, World Famous Fries, and the Egg McMuffin. The brand is the world's largest fast food chain, serving over 70 million customers daily in more than 100 countries. McDonald's is also recognized for its commitment to quality, service, cleanliness, and value.

Q

When was McDonald's founded?

McDonald's was founded in 1940 by Richard and Maurice McDonald in San Bernardino, California. The brand was later expanded globally by Ray Kroc, who purchased the company in 1961. McDonald's celebrated its 84th anniversary in 2024, marking over eight decades of innovation, growth, and cultural impact.

Q

What is the Big Mac?

The Big Mac is McDonald's signature burger, featuring two 100% beef patties, special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, and onions on a sesame seed bun. It was introduced in 1968 and has become one of the most iconic burgers in the world. Over 550 million Big Macs are sold annually, making it one of the best-selling food items of all time.

Q

Does McDonald's have a presence in Kenya?

Yes, McDonald's has a strong presence in Kenya with locations in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Eldoret. The brand has been serving Kenya since 1984, offering the full global menu alongside locally inspired items. McDonald's Kenya also offers McDelivery through the McDonald's app and delivery partners like Uber Eats and Glovo. M-Pesa payment is accepted at all locations.

Q

What is the McDonald's menu price range in Kenya?

McDonald's menu prices in Kenya vary by item. General price ranges in Kenyan Shillings (KES):

  • Value Menu: KES 200 – 400 (McDouble, McChicken, small fries)
  • Burgers: KES 400 – 900 (Big Mac, Quarter Pounder, Crispy Chicken)
  • Meals (with fries & drink): KES 600 – 1,500
  • Chicken McNuggets: KES 400 – 1,200 (4-20 pieces)
  • Breakfast: KES 300 – 700 (Egg McMuffin, Hotcakes)
  • Desserts & Beverages: KES 150 – 500 (McFlurry, McCafé, soft drinks)

Prices may vary by location and with promotional offers. Download the McDonald's app for exclusive deals and discounts.

Q

How can I order McDonald's delivery in Kenya?

You can order McDonald's delivery in Kenya through multiple channels:

  • McDonald's App: Download the official McDonald's app for ordering and exclusive offers
  • Uber Eats: Order through the Uber Eats app for delivery
  • Glovo: Available through the Glovo delivery platform
  • Direct call: Some locations offer direct phone ordering
  • In-store: Visit any McDonald's location for takeaway or dine-in

M-Pesa and credit/debit cards are accepted for delivery orders. Check the app for minimum order requirements and delivery fees.

Q

Does McDonald's offer vegetarian or plant-based options?

Yes, McDonald's offers vegetarian options in many markets, including:

  • McPlant: A plant-based burger available in select countries
  • Cheeseburger (without meat): Customizable vegetarian options
  • Fries: Cooked in vegetable oil (suitable for vegetarians)
  • Salads: Fresh salads with vegetarian dressings
  • Breakfast: Hash browns, hotcakes, and vegetarian breakfast wraps
  • Desserts: Ice cream, McFlurrys, pies, and sundaes

Plant-based options vary by location. Check the McDonald's app or website for vegetarian offerings at your local restaurant. In Kenya, vegetarian options are available by request.

Q

What is McDonald's sustainability initiative?

McDonald's sustainability initiatives focus on protecting the planet and serving communities. Key initiatives include:

  • 100% sustainable packaging: All packaging from renewable, recycled, or certified sources by 2025
  • Climate action: 50% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions by 2030, net-zero by 2050
  • Renewable energy: 80% of restaurants using renewable energy
  • Sustainable sourcing: Rainforest Alliance coffee, MSC certified fish, sustainable beef
  • Waste reduction: Recycling, composting, and food waste reduction programs
  • Ronald McDonald House Charities: Supporting families with children facing medical challenges

Q

What are McDonald's most popular menu items?

McDonald's most popular menu items include:

  • Big Mac: The iconic double-patty burger with special sauce – 550M+ sold annually
  • Chicken McNuggets: Tender, crispy chicken breast – 2.5B+ sold annually
  • World Famous Fries: Crispy, golden fries made from premium potatoes
  • Quarter Pounder with Cheese: Fresh ¼ lb beef with melted cheese
  • Egg McMuffin: The original breakfast sandwich with fresh egg
  • McFlurry: Creamy soft serve blended with toppings – introduced 1995
  • McCafé Coffee: Premium-roasted coffee available worldwide

These items have become global fast food icons, loved by customers in Kenya and around the world.

Q

Does McDonald's offer breakfast all day?

All-day breakfast availability varies by location. While McDonald's introduced all-day breakfast in many markets (including the US and UK), availability in Kenya depends on the specific restaurant. Most McDonald's locations in Kenya serve breakfast until 10:30 AM, though some may offer all-day options. Check with your local McDonald's for their breakfast hours. The McDonald's app shows updated hours and availability for each location.
