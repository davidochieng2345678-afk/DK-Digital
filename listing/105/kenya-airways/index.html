🔄

What Is a Codeshare?

A codeshare agreement allows Kenya Airways to place its "KQ" flight code on flights operated by partner airlines – and vice versa. This means you can book a single ticket for a journey that spans multiple airlines, with seamless baggage transfer, lounge access, and frequent flyer miles earned throughout.
