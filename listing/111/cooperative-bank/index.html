Co-operative Roots – Since 1965
Digital Banking – Innovation Leader

Co-operative Bank of Kenya is one of Kenya's largest banks, with a wide branch network across the country and a robust digital banking platform that serves millions of customers. Known for its cooperative roots and commitment to financial inclusion, Co-operative Bank has been a trusted financial partner for over 60 years, since its founding in 1965.

Founded in 1965, Co-operative Bank of Kenya was established as part of the cooperative movement in Kenya, with a mission to provide affordable financial services to cooperative societies and their members. Over the decades, the bank has grown exponentially, becoming a major player in Kenya's banking sector while staying true to its cooperative values of mutual assistance, democratic control, and community development. Today, Co-operative Bank is a fully-fledged commercial bank serving individuals, businesses, and corporations across the country.

Co-operative Bank's product portfolio is designed to meet the diverse needs of Kenyan customers. The bank offers a wide range of personal banking services including savings accounts, current accounts, personal loans, mortgages, and credit cards; business banking solutions such as corporate banking, trade finance, SME solutions, and treasury services; and digital banking through Co-op Kwa Jirani, mobile banking, and online platforms. The bank's M-Co-op Cash mobile money service provides convenient payment solutions to customers across Kenya.

Co-operative Bank is a leader in digital innovation in Kenya's banking sector. The bank's Co-op Kwa Jirani platform provides customers with secure, convenient, and real-time access to their finances. Through mobile banking and online platforms, customers can manage accounts, transfer funds, pay bills, apply for loans, and access financial advice – all from the palm of their hand. The bank's commitment to digital innovation ensures that customers enjoy a seamless banking experience that fits their busy lifestyles.

Co-operative Bank is deeply committed to financial inclusion and community development in Kenya. Through its cooperative roots, the bank supports cooperative societies, SMEs, and community-based organizations with tailored financial solutions and capacity building programs. The bank's commitment to social responsibility is reflected in its community engagement initiatives, financial literacy programs, and support for sustainable development across Kenya. Co-operative Bank is more than a bank – it's a partner in building a better future for all Kenyans.
