Since 1930 – 95+ Years of Excellence

Kakuzi PLC is a leading agricultural company in Kenya, specializing in the production and export of high-quality horticultural products including avocados, macadamia nuts, blueberries, and livestock. With a legacy spanning over 95 years, Kakuzi has established itself as a trusted name in Kenyan agriculture and global export markets.

Founded in 1930, Kakuzi PLC has grown from a small agricultural enterprise into a publicly traded company listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) under the ticker symbol KAKUZI. The company's commitment to quality, sustainability, and innovation has made it a preferred supplier for international buyers across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

Kakuzi's product portfolio includes premium Hass avocados known for their exceptional quality and rich flavor, high-quality macadamia nuts prized by international confectionery manufacturers, export-grade blueberries with superior nutritional value, and livestock including quality beef production and breeding programs. The company's export operations are supported by world-class processing facilities, certified quality management systems, and sustainable farming practices.

Kakuzi PLC is deeply committed to environmental sustainability and community development. The company implements eco-friendly farming practices, water conservation initiatives, and reforestation programs that protect Kenya's natural resources. Through its community engagement programs, Kakuzi supports local schools, healthcare facilities, and smallholder farmers, contributing to the socio-economic development of the Thika region and Kenya as a whole.

Kakuzi maintains international quality certifications including BRC Global Standards for food safety, GlobalG.A.P. for good agricultural practices, and organic certification for select products. These accreditations demonstrate Kakuzi's unwavering commitment to producing safe, high-quality agricultural products that meet the stringent requirements of global markets.

About Kakuzi PLC

Kenya's leading agricultural company, specializing in the production and export of premium horticultural products including avocados 🥑, macadamia nuts 🥜, blueberries 🫐, and livestock 🐄. Listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) and exporting to global markets since 1930.

Kakuzi PLC is a leading agricultural company in Kenya with a rich history dating back to 1930. Over the past 95 years, the company has evolved from a small agricultural enterprise into a publicly traded corporation listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) under the ticker symbol KAKUZI.

Today, Kakuzi PLC is a vertically integrated agricultural business specializing in the production, processing, and export of premium agricultural products. The company's operations span large-scale farming, state-of-the-art processing facilities, and a global distribution network that reaches customers across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

With a commitment to quality, sustainability, and innovation, Kakuzi has earned a reputation as a trusted supplier of Kenyan agricultural products to some of the world's most demanding international buyers. The company's certifications – including BRC Global Standards, GlobalG.A.P., and organic certification – reflect its unwavering commitment to food safety, quality assurance, and environmental responsibility.

Kakuzi PLC is also deeply committed to community development and environmental sustainability. The company supports local schools, healthcare facilities, and smallholder farmers in the Thika region, while implementing eco-friendly farming practices that protect Kenya's natural resources for future generations.
