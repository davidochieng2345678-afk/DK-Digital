At the heart of modern Kenyan farming lies the tractor – a versatile, powerful machine that has transformed agriculture from manual, labour‑intensive practices to efficient, mechanised operations. 🌱 Compact Tractors (10‑35 HP) – Perfect for Small‑Scale Farming

Compact tractors are the ideal choice for small‑scale farmers who need a versatile, manoeuvrable machine for a variety of tasks. With horsepower ranging from 10 to 35 HP, these tractors are perfect for farms up to 10 acres.

Best suited for:

  • Vegetable farming – ideal for small plots and greenhouse operations
  • Dairy farming – for feeding, cleaning, and light transport
  • Horticulture – flower farming, nursery operations
  • Small‑scale maize and bean farming
  • Land preparation – ploughing, harrowing, and tilling
  • Transport – moving feed, manure, and produce

Popular brands available at Domaki Motors: Mahindra (10‑35 HP range), Farmtrac (20‑35 HP), and selected Massey Ferguson models.

Price range in Kenya: KSh 800,000 – KSh 1,800,000

💡 Pro tip: Compact tractors are ideal for farmers transitioning from manual labour to mechanised farming. They are fuel‑efficient, easy to operate, and require minimal maintenance.

🌾 Utility Tractors (35‑80 HP) – The Backbone of Kenyan Farming

Utility tractors are the most popular tractors in Kenya, offering a perfect balance of power, versatility, and affordability. With horsepower ranging from 35 to 80 HP, these tractors are capable of handling a wide range of farming operations on farms up to 50 acres.

Best suited for:

  • Medium‑scale maize farming – ploughing, planting, and harvesting
  • Wheat farming – land preparation and cultivation
  • Dairy farming – feeding, manure management, and silage production
  • Horticulture – preparing beds and transporting produce

📖 Our Story – How DK Digital Directory Was Born

DK Digital Directory was founded in 2018 with a simple but powerful vision: to make it easy for Kenyan consumers to find and connect with trusted local businesses, and to give small and medium‑sized enterprises a powerful platform to grow their online presence.

The idea was born out of a real problem. Many businesses in Eldoret, the Rift Valley, and across Kenya were struggling to get noticed online. They had great products and services, but without a dedicated online presence, they were invisible to the growing number of customers who were searching for local solutions on their phones and computers.

At the same time, consumers were frustrated. They wanted to support local businesses, but finding reliable information – addresses, phone numbers, reviews, and service details – was a challenge. Social media pages were often incomplete, websites were hard to find, and directories were either outdated or full of spam.

DK Digital Directory was created to bridge that gap. We built a platform where businesses can create detailed, professional listings that showcase their products, services, and unique value. And we made it easy for customers to search, compare, and connect – all in one place.

Today, the directory features 50+ businesses across 20+ categories, attracts 1,000+ monthly visitors, and has helped countless local enterprises grow their customer base. From automotive dealerships like Domaki Motors to restaurants, legal firms, and agricultural suppliers – we are proud to be the go‑to resource for local discovery in Eldoret and beyond.

But we are just getting started. Our mission is to expand the directory to every major town in Kenya, connecting even more businesses with the customers who need them most.
