🏢 Business Identity & Brand Positioning

Domaki Motors is a premier automotive and agricultural machinery dealership located in the heart of Eldoret, Kenya. With a strong presence on Kisumu Road, next to the Shell Petrol Station, the company has established itself as a trusted one‑stop destination for quality used cars and new tractors. The business slogan, “Walk In, Drive Out,” is not just a tagline—it is a promise of efficiency, transparency, and customer satisfaction.

From the moment a potential buyer steps onto the lot, they are greeted by a diverse inventory that caters to both urban commuters and rural farmers. The showroom and open‑air yard display a rotating selection of vehicles, ranging from popular Japanese imports to locally used models, as well as brand‑new tractors designed for Kenya’s agricultural heartland. Domaki Motors bridges the gap between mobility and productivity, serving individual families, small‑scale farmers, and large agribusinesses alike.

The company’s dual focus on passenger vehicles and agricultural machinery sets it apart from traditional car yards. While many dealerships specialise in one niche, Domaki Motors understands that the modern Kenyan consumer often needs both a reliable car for daily commuting and a robust tractor for farm work. By offering both under one roof, the business saves customers time, money, and the hassle of dealing with multiple sellers.

Domaki Motors has built its reputation on integrity and after‑sales support. Every vehicle and tractor sold is thoroughly inspected, and the team provides clear documentation, honest condition reports, and transparent pricing. This commitment to ethical trading has earned the dealership a loyal customer base that spans across Eldoret, Kitale, Nakuru, and even as far as Kisumu and Nairobi.

The company’s staff are not just salespeople—they are knowledgeable advisors who understand the unique requirements of Kenyan roads and farming conditions. Whether advising on fuel‑efficient models for city driving or recommending tractor specifications for large‑scale wheat farming, the team at Domaki Motors ensures that every buyer leaves with a vehicle or machine that perfectly matches their needs.

📍 Strategic Location – Kisumu Road, Eldoret

Strategic location is one of the pillars of Domaki Motors’ success. Positioned on Kisumu Road in Eldoret, the yard is easily accessible from the town centre and major highways. The proximity to Shell Petrol Station is a convenient landmark that makes the dealership easy to find for both first‑time visitors and returning customers.

Eldoret itself is a rapidly growing city and a major commercial hub in the Rift Valley region. It serves as a gateway to agricultural areas such as Uasin Gishu, Trans‑Nzoia, and parts of Western Kenya. This location is ideal for a dealership that sells both cars and tractors, as it places Domaki Motors at the crossroads of urban and rural demand. Farmers from surrounding counties can drive in, inspect tractors, and arrange transport back to their farms, while city dwellers can browse the car collection without travelling far.

The business also benefits from high foot and vehicle traffic along Kisumu Road, ensuring constant visibility. The association with Shell, a well‑known brand, adds an extra layer of trust and credibility to the dealership. Customers often stop by after refuelling, making the location a natural point of discovery for those already in the area.

Accessibility extends beyond physical location. Domaki Motors offers flexible viewing hours, with staff available Monday through Saturday from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Ample parking space, a welcoming reception, and a customer‑friendly layout make the buying experience stress‑free. For those coming from out of town, the yard is just a short drive from the Eldoret‑Kisumu highway, with clear signage and directions provided over the phone.

The dealership also leverages its prime location to host open‑day events, where potential buyers can test‑drive vehicles and operate tractors under real conditions. These events have proven highly popular, drawing crowds from across the region and reinforcing Domaki Motors’ reputation as a community‑focused business.

🚗 Vehicle Inventory – Used Cars for Every Budget

One of the standout features of Domaki Motors is its extensive used car inventory, which includes both Japanese used and Kenyan used vehicles. This dual sourcing strategy gives customers a wide range of options in terms of price, condition, and mileage.

Japanese Used Cars are imported directly from Japan, where vehicles are meticulously maintained and have lower average mileage compared to their Kenyan counterparts. These cars are often in excellent condition, with comprehensive service histories and minimal wear. Popular models include Toyota (Premio, Allion, Vitz, Corolla, Land Cruiser), Nissan (X‑Trail, Note, Juke), Honda (Fit, Vezel), and Subaru (Forester, Outback). Each Japanese import undergoes a thorough inspection upon arrival to ensure it meets Domaki Motors’ quality standards.

Kenyan Used Cars are sourced from local owners who are upgrading or selling their vehicles. These cars are often well‑adapted to Kenyan roads and driving conditions, and they come at a more affordable price point. Many are single‑owner vehicles with complete service records. Domaki Motors carefully inspects each locally sourced car, performs any necessary repairs, and offers a limited warranty on select models.

Beyond Toyota, the dealership stocks all major brands including Isuzu, Mitsubishi, Ford, Mazda, Volkswagen, BMW, and Mercedes‑Benz. Whether a customer is looking for a fuel‑efficient saloon, a spacious family SUV, a rugged 4x4 for off‑road adventures, or a reliable pickup truck for business, Domaki Motors has something to suit every need and budget.

Each vehicle is presented with a detailed condition report, including photographs of the exterior, interior, and engine bay. Customers are encouraged to bring their own mechanic for inspection, and test drives are arranged on request. The dealership also offers flexible financing options, making it easier for buyers to afford their dream vehicle without financial strain.

Domaki Motors prides itself on transparency in pricing. There are no hidden fees, and every cost—from the purchase price to transfer fees—is discussed openly before any commitment is made. This approach has earned the dealership high marks in customer satisfaction and repeat business.

🚜 Agricultural Machinery – New Tractors for Kenyan Farmers

In addition to its passenger vehicle range, Domaki Motors is a leading supplier of new tractors in the Rift Valley region. The dealership partners with reputable tractor manufacturers to bring the latest agricultural technology to Kenyan farmers. These tractors are built to handle the diverse conditions of Kenyan farms, from the soft soils of the highlands to the tougher terrains of the lowlands.

The tractor inventory includes models suited for maize farming, wheat cultivation, dairy farming, horticulture, and large‑scale commercial agriculture. Key brands available include Massey Ferguson, John Deere, New Holland, and domestic favourites like the Farmtrac and Mahindra ranges. Each tractor is delivered brand‑new with full manufacturer warranties, genuine spare parts support, and after‑sales servicing.

Domaki Motors understands that buying a tractor is a significant investment for any farmer. That is why the dealership offers comprehensive farm consultations, helping buyers choose the right horsepower, transmission type, and implement compatibility. The team also assists with financing through partner banks and microfinance institutions, making it easier for farmers to acquire the machinery they need to boost productivity.

After‑sales support is a cornerstone of the agricultural division. The dealership stocks a wide range of genuine spare parts, and its workshop is staffed by trained mechanics who can perform routine maintenance, major repairs, and emergency breakdown assistance. This ensures minimal downtime for farmers during critical planting and harvesting seasons.

Domaki Motors also organises regular tractor demonstration days, where farmers can test different models on actual farm plots. These events are highly educational, allowing farmers to see the machines in action and make informed decisions based on real‑world performance.

🔧 Quality Assurance & After‑Sales Support

At Domaki Motors, quality assurance begins before a vehicle or tractor ever reaches the showroom. Every unit undergoes a rigorous multi‑point inspection covering engine performance, transmission, brakes, suspension, electrical systems, and bodywork. Any issues identified are repaired using genuine parts, ensuring that every sale is backed by reliability.

The dealership’s workshop is equipped with modern diagnostic tools and staffed by experienced technicians who receive ongoing training on the latest automotive and agricultural technologies. Whether it’s a routine oil change, a complex engine overhaul, or a tractor transmission rebuild, the workshop delivers prompt, professional service.

Domaki Motors also offers extended warranty options on many vehicles and tractors, giving buyers extra peace of mind. For used cars, the standard warranty covers major mechanical components for up to 3 months or 5,000 kilometres, whichever comes first. Tractors come with the manufacturer’s full warranty, which can be extended upon request.

Customer support extends beyond the sale. The dealership maintains a dedicated helpdesk that assists with registration, insurance, and any post‑purchase queries. Clients are also invited to join the Domaki Motors owners’ community, which provides tips, maintenance schedules, and exclusive offers on parts and services.

The commitment to quality and support has resulted in a stellar reputation, with many customers returning to upgrade their vehicles or buy additional tractors. Word‑of‑mouth referrals remain a significant source of new business, a testament to the trust Domaki Motors has built in the community.

💰 Financing & Payment Options

Understanding that cash purchases are not always feasible, Domaki Motors has partnered with multiple financial institutions to offer flexible financing solutions. Whether you are a salaried individual, a small‑business owner, or a large‑scale farmer, there is a payment plan designed to fit your budget.

Options include hire purchase, bank loans, and asset financing specifically tailored for agricultural machinery. The dealership’s finance team works closely with clients to guide them through the application process, ensuring that paperwork is complete and approvals are obtained swiftly. In many cases, pre‑approval can be arranged before a customer even selects a vehicle or tractor.

Down payments are flexible, and repayment periods can extend up to 48 months for passenger vehicles and up to 60 months for tractors. Interest rates are competitive, and the dealership maintains transparency regarding all fees and charges. There are no hidden processing fees, and early repayment is always welcomed without penalty.

For buyers who prefer to pay in cash, Domaki Motors offers attractive discounts on selected models. The dealership also accepts payments via mobile money (M‑Pesa), bank transfers, and debit/credit cards, making transactions convenient and secure.

🤝 Community Engagement & Corporate Responsibility

Domaki Motors is more than a business—it is a responsible corporate citizen deeply rooted in the Eldoret community. The dealership actively participates in local development initiatives, including sponsorship of agricultural training programmes, support for local schools, and contributions to road safety campaigns.

Each year, the dealership hosts a “Farmers’ Day” event, where agricultural experts are invited to share insights on modern farming techniques, crop rotation, and tractor maintenance. These events are free to attend and have become a staple in the region’s agricultural calendar.

Domaki Motors also supports young entrepreneurs by offering discounted rates and mentorship to first‑time buyers, particularly those venturing into agribusiness. By empowering the next generation of farmers and business owners, the dealership is investing in the long‑term prosperity of the region.

The company maintains a safe and welcoming environment for all visitors, with accessibility features for persons with disabilities. It also promotes environmental sustainability by encouraging proper disposal of used oil and batteries, and by advocating for fuel‑efficient vehicle choices that reduce carbon emissions.

Through these community efforts, Domaki Motors has become a trusted name not only in automotive and agricultural sales but also in social development. Customers feel good about supporting a business that gives back, reinforcing their loyalty and advocacy.

At the same time, consumers were frustrated. They wanted to support local businesses, but finding reliable information – addresses, phone numbers, reviews, and service details – was a challenge. Social media pages were often incomplete, websites were hard to find, and directories were either outdated or full of spam.

DK Digital Directory was created to bridge that gap. We built a platform where businesses can create detailed, professional listings that showcase their products, services, and unique value. And we made it easy for customers to search, compare, and connect – all in one place.

Today, the directory features 50+ businesses across 20+ categories, attracts 1,000+ monthly visitors, and has helped countless local enterprises grow their customer base. From automotive dealerships like Domaki Motors to restaurants, legal firms, and agricultural suppliers – we are proud to be the go‑to resource for local discovery in Eldoret and beyond.

But we are just getting started. Our mission is to expand the directory to every major town in Kenya, connecting even more businesses with the customers who need them most.
