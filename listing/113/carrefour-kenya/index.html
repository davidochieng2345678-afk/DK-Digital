About Carrefour Kenya

Carrefour Kenya is a French multinational retail chain that has become a go-to destination for quality groceries, fresh produce, household items, electronics, fashion, and pharmacy products in Kenya. As part of the Carrefour Group, one of the world's largest retail chains, Carrefour Kenya delivers a world-class shopping experience with competitive prices and a wide selection of products at convenient locations across Nairobi and major Kenyan cities.

Carrefour is a French multinational retail corporation with a strong and growing presence in Kenya. The chain entered the Kenyan market in 2016, opening its first store at The Junction Shopping Centre in Nairobi. Since then, Carrefour has expanded rapidly, establishing multiple stores across the country and becoming a favorite destination for Kenyan shoppers seeking quality products, competitive prices, and a pleasant shopping experience.

Globally, Carrefour is one of the world's largest retail chains, operating thousands of stores across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas. The Carrefour Group is known for its commitment to quality, innovation in retail, and dedication to customer satisfaction. In Kenya, Carrefour brings this global expertise to local communities, offering a world-class shopping experience that combines the best of international retail with a deep understanding of local needs and preferences.

Carrefour Kenya's product range is designed to meet the diverse needs of Kenyan families and individuals. The chain offers a wide selection of fresh groceries including fruits, vegetables, meat, and bakery items; household essentials such as cleaning products, kitchenware, and home essentials; electronics including TVs, phones, laptops, and accessories; fashion for all ages including clothing, footwear, and accessories; and a pharmacy section with health and wellness products.

Carrefour Kenya is also committed to sustainability and community engagement. The chain works with local suppliers to source fresh produce and quality products, supporting Kenyan farmers and businesses. Through its corporate social responsibility programs, Carrefour contributes to community development, environmental sustainability, and social welfare initiatives across Kenya.
