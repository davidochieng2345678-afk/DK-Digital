The Standard Group is Kenya's oldest media house, with a rich history of journalistic excellence dating back to 1902. For over 120 years, the Standard Group has been a trusted source of news and information for millions of Kenyans, delivering quality journalism across print, broadcast, and digital platforms.

Founded in 1902, the Standard Group began its journey with The Standard newspaper – Kenya's oldest continuously published newspaper. Over the decades, the Group has expanded its reach and influence, becoming a multi-platform media powerhouse that includes print newspapers, television channels, radio stations, and a leading digital news platform. The Standard Group has played a pivotal role in Kenya's development, democracy, and cultural identity, chronicling the nation's journey from colonial times to independence and beyond.

Today, the Standard Group is a fully integrated media company with a diverse portfolio of media brands. Its print division publishes The Standard, The Standard on Saturday, and The Standard on Sunday – reaching millions of readers across Kenya. Its television division operates KTN News (Kenya Television Network), KTN Home, and KTN Farmers TV, delivering breaking news, current affairs, and specialized content to audiences nationwide. Its radio division includes Radio Maisha, Spice FM, and Gukena FM, reaching listeners across the country. Standard Digital, the Group's online platform, provides real-time news and analysis to a growing digital audience.

Trust and credibility are the cornerstones of the Standard Group's journalistic tradition. The Group is committed to the highest standards of journalism, including accuracy, fairness, independence, and accountability. Its editorial team of experienced journalists and reporters works tirelessly to inform, educate, and empower Kenyans with reliable and relevant news and information. The Standard Group is also a strong advocate for press freedom and media independence in Kenya and across Africa.

As the media landscape continues to evolve, the Standard Group remains at the forefront of innovation, adapting to new technologies and audience behaviors. The Group is investing in digital transformation, data-driven journalism, and audience engagement to ensure that it continues to serve Kenyans with quality journalism for the next 120 years and beyond. The Standard Group is also expanding its reach across East Africa, with a vision to become the region's leading media organization.

About Standard Group

The Standard Group is Kenya's oldest media house, founded in 1902 with over 120 years of journalistic excellence. As a multi-platform media company, the Standard Group delivers trusted news and information to millions of Kenyans across print, television, radio, and digital platforms.

The Standard Group is a cornerstone of Kenya's media landscape, with a rich heritage that dates back to 1902. As Kenya's oldest media house, the Standard Group has chronicled the nation's journey from colonial times to independence and beyond, earning a reputation for trusted journalism, editorial independence, and public service.

The Standard Group's flagship publication, The Standard, is Kenya's oldest continuously published newspaper, with a circulation that reaches millions of Kenyans daily. Over the years, the Group has expanded from print journalism into a fully integrated media company, with a diverse portfolio that includes television channels, radio stations, and a leading digital news platform.

The Standard Group's television division operates KTN News (Kenya Television Network), KTN Home, and KTN Farmers TV. KTN News is a leading news channel with 24/7 coverage of breaking news, current affairs, and in-depth analysis. KTN Farmers TV is a specialized channel dedicated to Kenya's agricultural sector, providing farming news, market updates, and agribusiness tips to Kenyan farmers.

The Standard Group's radio division includes Radio Maisha, a popular Swahili station with news, music, and talk programming; Spice FM, an urban contemporary radio station; and Gukena FM, a Kikuyu-language station serving the Central Kenya region. Standard Digital, the Group's online platform, provides real-time news, video content, podcasts, and interactive features to a growing digital audience.

KTN News (Kenya Television Network) is a leading Kenyan television news channel owned by the Standard Group. The channel provides 24/7 news coverage, breaking news, current affairs, and in-depth analysis.

KTN News also operates KTN Home for lifestyle and entertainment content, and KTN Farmers TV, a specialized channel dedicated to Kenya's agricultural sector. The channel is committed to accurate, fair, and independent journalism.

Q

📻 What radio stations does Standard Group operate?

The Standard Group operates three radio stations that serve diverse audiences across Kenya:

  • 📻 Radio Maisha: Popular Swahili station with news, music, and talk programming
  • 🎵 Spice FM: Urban contemporary radio station targeting younger audiences
  • 📻 Gukena FM: Kikuyu-language station serving the Central Kenya region

Together, these stations reach millions of listeners across the country with engaging content in multiple languages.

Q

💻 What is Standard Digital?

Standard Digital is the online home of the Standard Group, delivering real-time news, breaking stories, video content, podcasts, and interactive features to a growing digital audience.

Available at standardmedia.co.ke on desktop, mobile, and social media platforms, Standard Digital offers live streaming of KTN News and radio stations, making it a one-stop destination for news, entertainment, and information.

Q

🌾 Does Standard Group have a farmers' channel?

Yes, Standard Group operates KTN Farmers TV, a specialized television channel dedicated to Kenya's agricultural sector.

KTN Farmers TV provides farming news, market updates, agribusiness tips, farmer success stories, and expert advice on crop farming, livestock, horticulture, and agribusiness. The channel is committed to empowering Kenyan farmers with the knowledge they need to improve productivity and increase profitability.
