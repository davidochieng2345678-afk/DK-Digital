Who We Are

Nation Media Group (NMG) is the largest independent media house in East and Central Africa. Founded in 1959, NMG has grown from a single newspaper into a multimedia powerhouse with a presence in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda.

With a mission to inform, educate, and entertain, NMG is a cornerstone of African journalism. The group is committed to independent, objective, and high‑quality reporting that serves the public interest.

What Is a Dofollow Backlink?

A dofollow backlink is a hyperlink from one website to another that passes SEO authority (also known as "link juice"). When Google sees a dofollow link from a trusted, high‑authority domain to your website, it interprets this as a vote of confidence. This helps your website rank higher in search results.

Unlike nofollow links, which tell search engines not to pass authority, dofollow links actively contribute to your website's Domain Authority (DA) and Page Authority (PA). This is why dofollow backlinks are considered the gold standard in SEO.

Nation Media Group (NMG) is the largest independent media house in East and Central Africa. Founded in 1959, NMG operates print, digital, and broadcast platforms across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda. It is the publisher of Daily Nation, The East African, Taifa Leo, and other leading titles.

Nation Media Group was founded in 1959 as the Nation Group of Companies. It started with the Daily Nation newspaper and has since grown into East Africa's largest independent media house.

Nation Media Group operates in four countries across East Africa:

  • Kenya – Headquarters

Nation Media Group owns two major television stations:
