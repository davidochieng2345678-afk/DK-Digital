The Challenge: "I Think My Agency Is Using the Same Strategy for Everyone"

Dr. Faith Akinyi Omondi founded SaaSify Technologies in 2022. Her company provides software solutions for SMEs across Kenya. By 2024, she knew she needed a strong online presence to compete in Nairobi's crowded tech market.

In October 2023, Faith hired an SEO agency that promised results. They charged her KES 30,000 per month for 10 months — a total of KES 300,000.

But after several months, Faith began to notice something troubling. She looked at the agency's other clients and realized they were all getting the same content, the same keywords, and the same strategy — regardless of their industry.

A fintech startup was getting the same blog topics as a logistics company. A healthcare SaaS was getting the same recommendations as a retail software provider. Everyone was getting the same generic strategy.

Her suspicions were confirmed:

  • Generic blog posts that could apply to any business
  • Broad keywords like "software solutions" that were impossible to rank for
  • No targeting of specific SaaS or tech keywords
  • No focus on Nairobi or the Kenyan market
  • Confusing reports with "impressions" and "clicks" but no real leads

Faith felt cheated. She had paid KES 300,000 for a cookie-cutter strategy that was never going to work for her business. She was ready to abandon SEO entirely.

Faith was cautiously optimistic. She agreed to give SEO one more chance — this time with a custom strategy.
