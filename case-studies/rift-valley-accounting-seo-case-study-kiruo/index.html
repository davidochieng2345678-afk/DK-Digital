The Challenge: Beautiful Reports With No Real Results

Esther Chepkorir Kiptoo founded Rift Valley Accounting Solutions in Kapsoya, Eldoret in 2020. She provided accounting, tax, and business consulting services to SMEs and individuals across the Rift Valley region.

In January 2024, Esther hired an SEO agency that promised to put her website on Page 1 of Google. They charged her KES 20,000 per month for 9 months — a total of KES 180,000.

Every month, the agency sent her a report. Beautiful reports. Full of colorful graphs, impressive-looking charts, and technical terms that sounded important:

  • "Impressions: 5,200"
  • "Clicks: 312"
  • "Average Position: 4.2"
  • "Keyword Volume: 1,800"

But Esther noticed something troubling: her phone wasn't ringing. No new client inquiries. No new consultations. Her business wasn't growing.

She began to suspect the truth:

  • The "impressions" didn't mean people were finding her
  • The "clicks" didn't mean people were becoming clients
  • The "average position" didn't mean she was visible for keywords that mattered
  • She was paying for vanity metrics — and getting no real results

Esther felt cheated. She had paid KES 180,000 for reports that looked good but meant nothing. She was ready to give up on digital marketing entirely.
