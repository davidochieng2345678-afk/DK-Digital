Ali was frustrated. He felt cheated. He was ready to abandon SEO entirely and focus only on social media ads and word-of-mouth.

In September 2025, Ali booked a free strategy session with DK Digital SEO. During the session, we conducted a comprehensive audit of Cosmik Mombasa's website, SEO strategy, and competition.
