The Challenge: "SEO Takes Two Years — I Don't Have That Kind of Time"

Peter Odhiambo Ochieng opened Lake Basin Hardware & Construction in Kibuye, Kisumu in 2018. The business grew steadily through word-of-mouth and local reputation. But by 2023, Peter noticed something troubling: competitors were appearing on Google, and customers were finding them online instead of walking into his store.

In early 2024, Peter hired an SEO agency that promised results. They told him: "SEO takes time. You need to be patient. It usually takes about two years to see significant results."

Peter paid the agency KES 20,000 per month for 6 months — a total of KES 120,000. He waited patiently. He watched his analytics dashboard. He hoped for the best.

But nothing happened.

  • His website remained on Page 4 or worse for all keywords
  • His traffic stayed below 15 visitors per day
  • No new customer inquiries from organic search
  • The agency sent vague reports with no actionable insights

Peter was frustrated. He felt like he had wasted KES 120,000 and 6 months of time. He told himself: "SEO is too slow. I can't afford to wait two years. My business needs customers now."

He stopped paying the agency and resigned himself to competing on price and location alone. His business continued to struggle.

Then, in November 2024, a friend recommended DK Digital SEO. Peter was skeptical but decided to have a conversation.

The Solution: A Different Approach to SEO

During the free strategy session, Peter shared his frustration. "I was told SEO takes two years. I don't have two years. I can't wait that long."

We explained that his previous agency was wrong. SEO doesn't take two years — not when it's done right. With the right strategy, significant results can be achieved in 90 days.

  • No Local Keyword Strategy: The previous agency targeted broad terms like "hardware store" instead of local terms like "best hardware store Kibuye Kisumu"
  • Poor Technical Foundation: The website was slow, not mobile-friendly, and had broken links
  • No Content: The website had 0 blog posts and minimal product descriptions
  • No Local Citations: The business was not listed on any local directories
  • No Backlinks: Zero links from any Kenyan or Kisumu-based websites

We proposed a focused, 90-day strategy:

  • Optimize existing pages with local keywords
  • Create 6 blog posts targeting Kisumu hardware and construction keywords

  • Build backlinks from Kisumu-based websites
  • Create a content calendar with seasonal topics

Peter was still skeptical, but he agreed to try. "I've been burned before. But I'll give you 90 days to prove me wrong."

The Implementation: 90 Days of Focused Action

Implementation began on December 1, 2024. Here's the step-by-step breakdown:

Weeks 1-2: Foundation & Technical Fixes

  • Fixed website speed: reduced load time from 4.5 seconds to 1.8 seconds
  • Made the site fully mobile-responsive

Weeks 3-4: Content Creation & On-Page Optimization

  • Published first 3 blog posts: "Best Hardware Store in Kisumu," "Building Materials Guide for Kisumu," and "Top 5 Construction Tips for Lake Basin"

  • Submitted updated sitemap to Google Search Console

Weeks 5-8: Content Scaling & Local Citations

  • Published 3 more blog posts: "Affordable Building Materials in Kisumu," "DIY Construction Tips," and "How to Choose the Right Hardware Store"

Weeks 9-12: Acceleration & Growth

  • "best hardware store Kibuye Kisumu" reached #1 on Page 1

  • Monthly sales increased by 200%
  • Peter began receiving inquiries from construction companies outside Kisumu
