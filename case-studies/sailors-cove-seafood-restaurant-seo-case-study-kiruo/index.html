The Challenge: A Skeptical Restaurant Owner Who Had Been Burned Before

Hassan Omar Abdulrahman opened Sailor's Cove Seafood Restaurant in Nyali, Mombasa in 2022. Located near the beach, the restaurant had beautiful views and delicious food. But Hassan struggled to fill tables during weekdays. Weekends were busy, but Monday through Thursday were often dead.

In early 2024, Hassan hired an SEO agency that promised him "#1 rankings on Google" and "a flood of new customers." He paid them KES 15,000 per month for 10 months — a total of KES 150,000.

But the results never came.

  • His website traffic remained stagnant at under 20 visitors per day
  • His phone barely rang with new reservations
  • He never ranked for keywords like "best seafood restaurant Mombasa"
  • The agency sent confusing reports with "impressions" and "clicks" that meant nothing
  • When he asked questions, they gave vague answers

Hassan felt cheated. He was embarrassed that he had been taken advantage of. He told himself: "SEO is a scam. It's for big companies with big budgets. Small businesses like mine can't afford to play this game."

He stopped paying the agency and focused on social media ads and word-of-mouth instead.

Then, in February 2025, Hassan received an email from DK Digital SEO. It wasn't a generic sales pitch — it was a personalized message that acknowledged his previous experience with SEO and offered a free, no-obligation strategy session. Hassan was skeptical, but something made him respond.

2

The Solution: Proving SEO Wasn't a Scam

During the free strategy session, we didn't make any grand promises. We didn't guarantee "#1 rankings" or "10,000 visitors in 30 days." Instead, we did something different: we showed Hassan exactly what was wrong with his website and explained what we would do to fix it.

What we discovered:

  • No Local SEO: The previous agency targeted generic keywords like "seafood restaurant" instead of local terms like "best seafood restaurant Nyali Mombasa"
  • Poor Website Experience: The website loaded slowly, was not mobile-friendly, and had confusing navigation
  • No Content: There were only 2 blog posts on the entire site, both written in 2023
  • No Local Backlinks: No links from any Mombasa-based or Kenyan websites
  • No Google My Business Optimization: The restaurant's Google My Business profile was incomplete and unoptimized

We proposed a custom strategy:

  • Optimize the Google My Business profile with photos, hours, and customer reviews
  • Create 6 blog posts targeting Mombasa restaurant keywords
  • Fix technical issues: speed up the website and make it mobile-friendly
  • Build local backlinks from Mombasa food blogs and travel websites
  • List the restaurant on local directories and food review sites
  • Create a content calendar with seasonal promotions and events

Hassan was still skeptical, but he agreed to give SEO one more chance. He told us: "I've been burned before. If this doesn't work, I'm done with SEO forever."

The Implementation: 90 Days of Transformation

Implementation began on March 1, 2025. Here's the step-by-step breakdown of what we did:

Weeks 1-2: Foundation & Local SEO

  • Fully optimized Google My Business profile with professional photos, menu items, and operating hours
  • Encouraged 5 existing customers to leave Google reviews
  • Conducted keyword research focused on Mombasa and Nyali restaurant searches
  • Fixed technical issues: improved page speed by 50% and made the site fully mobile-responsive

Weeks 3-4: Content Creation & On-Page Optimization

  • Published first 3 blog posts: "Best Seafood Restaurants in Mombasa," "Romantic Dinner Spots in Nyali," and "Why Sailor's Cove is Mombasa's Hidden Gem"
  • Optimized all service pages with local keywords
  • Added schema markup for restaurant and local business
  • Submitted updated sitemap to Google Search Console

Weeks 5-8: Content Scaling & Link Building

  • Published 3 more blog posts: "Top 5 Seafood Dishes to Try in Mombasa," "Family Dining in Nyali," and "Sunset Dining at Sailor's Cove"
  • Secured 4 backlinks from Mombasa food blogs and travel websites
  • Listed the restaurant on 5 local directories
  • Early ranking improvements: "best seafood restaurant Mombasa" moved from Page 6 to Page 3

Weeks 9-12: Acceleration & Growth

  • Published final blog posts: "What Makes Sailor's Cove Different" and "Behind the Scenes at Sailor's Cove"
  • Website traffic doubled
  • Ranked #2 for "best seafood restaurant Mombasa" and #1 for "romantic dinner Nyali"
  • Phone was ringing with new reservations every day
  • Weekday bookings increased by 180%
