Tusker is Kenya's original beer, brewed since 1922 by East African Breweries Limited (EABL). It is the fastest‑growing brand in Kenya with a 67% value increase to KSh 9.6 billion and a Brand Strength Index (BSI) score of 97.1 out of 100.

Named after founder George Hurst's legendary elephant, Tusker has become a beloved Kenyan icon, representing quality, heritage, and the spirit of Kenya.

Key products and services:

  • Tusker Lager: The original premium Kenyan beer
  • Tusker Malt: A richer, smoother brew
  • Tusker Cider: Crisp apple cider for a modern taste
  • EABL Portfolio: Kenya Cane, Johnnie Walker, Guinness, and more
  • Corporate Responsibility: Sustainability, water conservation, and community programs
