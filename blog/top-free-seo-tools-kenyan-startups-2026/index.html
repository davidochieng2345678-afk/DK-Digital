Google Search Console – Your Website's Health Monitor

The most important free SEO tool for any Kenyan startup. Google Search Console (GSC) is your direct line to Google – it tells you exactly how Google sees your website.

🔍

What Is Google Search Console?

Google Search Console is a free tool from Google that helps you monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot your site's presence in Google Search results. It's the only tool that gives you first-party data directly from Google – no estimates, no guesses, just real data.

✅ What GSC Shows You:

  • Which keywords bring traffic to your site
  • Indexing issues (why pages aren't on Google)
  • Mobile usability problems
  • Backlinks pointing to your site
  • Click-through rates (CTR) for each keyword
  • Core Web Vitals and site speed

🎯 Why Startups Need GSC:

  • Free – No cost, unlimited data
  • Direct from Google – 100% accurate data
  • Essential – You can't do SEO without it
  • Actionable – Tells you exactly what to fix
  • Growth-focused – Track your progress over time

⚙️ How to Set Up Google Search Console in 5 Minutes

Setting up GSC is quick and free. Follow these steps to get started:

1.

Go to Google Search Console

Visit search.google.com/search-console and sign in with your Google account.

2.

Add Your Website

Click "Add Property" and enter your website URL. Choose the domain property (recommended) or URL prefix.

3.

Verify Ownership

Verify your site using one of the methods: HTML file upload, HTML tag, Google Analytics, or DNS record.

4.

Submit Your Sitemap

Go to "Sitemaps" and submit your sitemap URL (e.g., sitemap.xml). This helps Google find all your pages.

5.

Request Indexing for New Pages

Use the URL Inspection tool to request indexing for new or updated pages. This speeds up Google's discovery.

6.

Start Exploring

Within 24-48 hours, you'll start seeing data. Check the "Performance" report to see your first keywords and impressions.

Pro Tip: If you have a Shopify store, you can verify GSC through your Shopify admin. Go to "Online Store" → "Preferences" → "Google Search Console" and follow the prompts.

What Is Google Analytics?

Google Analytics (GA4) is a free web analytics tool that tracks and reports website traffic. It's the most powerful free tool for understanding your audience – where they come from, what they do on your site, and how to convert them into customers.

✅ What GA4 Shows You:

⚙️ How to Set Up Google Analytics in 10 Minutes

GA4 is the latest version of Google Analytics. Here's how to set it up for your startup.

1.

Create a Google Analytics Account

Go to analytics.google.com and sign in with your Google account. Click "Start measuring" to create a new account.

2.

Set Up a Property

Enter your website name, URL, and industry category. Choose the reporting time zone and currency for your location (Kenya).

3.

Get Your Tracking Code

Copy the global site tag (gtag.js) or measurement ID. This is the code you'll add to your website.

4.

Install the Tracking Code

Add the code to every page of your website. For Shopify: go to "Online Store" → "Preferences" → "Google Analytics" and paste your measurement ID.

5.

Set Up Conversions (Goals)

Define what matters to your startup – form submissions, purchases, WhatsApp clicks, or newsletter signups. Track these as conversions.

6.

Connect to Google Search Console

Link your GA4 property to GSC for richer data. Go to "Admin" → "Product Links" → "Google Search Console".

Pro Tip: If you're using WordPress, install the "Site Kit by Google" plugin – it automatically sets up GA4, GSC, and PageSpeed Insights in one click.

⚙️ How to Use Ubersuggest for Keyword Research

Ubersuggest is simple and powerful. Here's how to use it effectively for your Kenyan startup.

1.

Go to Ubersuggest

Visit ubersuggest.com and enter your keyword or topic. For startups, use broad terms like "SEO Kenya" or "digital marketing Nairobi."

2.

Check Keyword Ideas

Ubersuggest will show you hundreds of related keywords. Look for keywords with decent search volume (100+) and low difficulty (KD under 40).

3.

Analyze Search Volume & Difficulty

Pay attention to two metrics: Search Volume (how many people search) and Keyword Difficulty (how hard it is to rank). Start with low-difficulty keywords.

4.

Look at Content Ideas

Click the "Content Ideas" tab to see what pages are already ranking for your keyword. Use these as inspiration for your own content.

5.

Export Your Keywords

Export your keyword list as a CSV file. This helps you organize and track your keyword strategy over time.

6.

Repeat for 3-5 Seed Keywords

Use 3-5 seed keywords to build a comprehensive keyword list. For a startup, 50-100 keywords is a good starting point.

Pro Tip: Use Ubersuggest's free 3 searches strategically. Focus on your most important topics first. You can also create multiple accounts for more searches.

📊 Key Ubersuggest Metrics to Understand

📈

Search Volume

The number of people searching for a keyword per month.

100-500 = Good 500-1000 = Better 1000+ = Competitive
🎯

Keyword Difficulty (KD)

How hard it is to rank for a keyword. Lower is better for startups.

0-30 = Easy 30-50 = Medium 50+ = Hard
💡

Content Ideas

Pages already ranking for your keyword. Shows you what type of content Google prefers.

Blog posts Product pages Guides

Startup Strategy: Target keywords with Search Volume 100-500 and Keyword Difficulty under 30. These are the "low-hanging fruit" for Kenyan startups.

🔍 Key Issues to Look For in Your Crawl

🔗

Broken Links (404 Errors)

Links that lead to pages that don't exist. These create a poor user experience and waste link authority.

Fix: Redirect broken links or update them to the correct URL.

📄

Duplicate Meta Titles & Descriptions

When multiple pages have the same title or description, Google gets confused about which page to rank.

Fix: Write unique meta titles and descriptions for every page.

🏷️

Missing Meta Tags

Pages without meta titles or descriptions won't perform well in search results.

Fix: Add meta titles and descriptions to all pages.

🖼️

Missing Alt Text on Images

Images without alt text are invisible to screen readers and miss out on image search traffic.

Fix: Add descriptive alt text to all images.

🔄

Redirect Chains

A chain of multiple redirects (e.g., Page A → Page B → Page C). This slows down page load and wastes authority.

Fix: Replace redirect chains with direct links.

📊

Duplicate Content

Similar content on multiple pages. Google struggles to know which page to show in search results.

Fix: Use canonical tags or consolidate duplicate content.

Startup Strategy: Fix "Critical" and "High" priority issues first. These have the biggest impact on your rankings and user experience.

⚠️ Common Canva Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Using Too Many Fonts

Stick to 2-3 fonts maximum. Too many fonts look unprofessional and confusing.

❌ Overcrowding Your Design

White space is your friend. Don't cram too much information into one design.

❌ Using Low-Quality Images

Blurry or pixelated images look unprofessional. Always use high-quality images.

❌ Ignoring Brand Consistency

Use the same colors, fonts, and logo on all designs to build brand recognition.

Pro Tip: Create a "Brand Kit" in Canva with your logo, brand colors, and fonts. This ensures every design is consistent with your brand identity.
