🔍 Step 1: Get Found — Make Sure Google Can Find You

If Google can't find your website, it can't rank it. The first step is making sure your site is discoverable, crawlable, and indexable.

Think of it like this: You can have the best content in the world, but if your website is hidden in a dark alley with no sign, no one will find it. The Kiruo Method starts by putting up a big, bright sign that Google can see.

What to Do:

  • Submit your sitemap to Google — This tells Google about all the pages on your site. Use Google Search Console.
  • Fix 404 errors — Broken pages confuse Google and waste crawl budget. Redirect them to relevant pages.
  • Check your robots.txt — Make sure you're not accidentally blocking important pages from being crawled.
  • Remove noindex tags — If you've accidentally told Google not to index your pages, remove those tags.
  • Request indexing — Use Google Search Console's URL Inspection tool to request indexing for your most important pages.

💡 Quick Win:

Log into Google Search Console and check your Coverage report. If you see "Crawled - currently not indexed," those pages need attention. Request indexing for them today.

👀 Step 2: Get Noticed — Show Up for What People Search

Once Google can find you, you need to show up for relevant searches. This means targeting the right keywords and optimizing your pages to rank for them.

The key is relevance. You want to show up when people search for exactly what you offer. Not too broad. Not too narrow. Just right.

What to Do:

  • Do simple keyword research — What are your customers searching for? Use free tools like Google's "People Also Ask" or Ubersuggest.
  • Target long-tail keywords — Instead of "coffee shop," target "best coffee shop in Kilimani." Less competition, higher conversion.
  • Optimize your title tags — Include your primary keyword and make it compelling. 50-60 characters is ideal.
  • Optimize your meta descriptions — Include keywords and a call-to-action. 150-160 characters is ideal.
  • Use headings properly — One H1 per page (your main title), H2s for sections, H3s for sub-sections. Include keywords naturally.

🤝 Step 3: Get Trusted — Build Authority and Credibility

Google ranks trustworthy sites higher. The third step is building authority and credibility — so Google (and customers) know they can trust you.

Think of it like this: Would you rather buy from a business everyone recommends, or one you've never heard of? Google works the same way. Trust is everything.

What to Do:

  • Create quality content — Publish helpful, informative content that solves problems for your customers. Blog posts, guides, videos.
  • Get customer reviews — Ask happy customers to leave Google reviews. More reviews = more trust.
  • Build local citations — List your business on Kenyan directories (Kenya Business Directory, Yellow Pages Kenya, etc.).
  • Earn backlinks — When other websites link to you, it's like a "vote of confidence." Guest posts, partnerships, and directory listings help.
  • Be consistent — Make sure your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) are the same everywhere online.

💡 Quick Win:

Ask 5 happy customers to leave a Google review today. Each review builds trust and improves your local search rankings.

🛒 Step 4: Get Chosen — Convert Visitors into Customers

Traffic without conversions is wasted. The final step is making sure that when people visit your website, they actually become customers.

Think of it like this: You've brought people to your store. Now you need to make it easy for them to buy. Clear signs, helpful staff, and a simple checkout process.

What to Do:

  • Use clear calls-to-action (CTAs) — Tell visitors exactly what to do next. "Call Now," "Get a Free Quote," "Book Today."
  • Make it easy to contact you — Phone number, email, and WhatsApp should be visible on every page.
  • Simplify your forms — Only ask for essential information. Each extra field reduces conversions by 10-20%.
  • Optimize for mobile — Most Kenyan users are on mobile. Make sure your site works perfectly on phones.
  • Add trust signals — Testimonials, reviews, certifications, and guarantees build confidence.

✅ Quick Win:

Add a "Call Now" button to your website header on mobile. Make it easy for customers to reach you with one tap.

Simple Implementation Guide

📋 The Action Plan: Step-by-step instructions to implement The Kiruo Method in your business

The Kiruo Method is simple — but it still needs to be implemented. This section provides a step-by-step action plan that any Kenyan business owner can follow to rank higher on Google.

You don't need to be a technical expert. You don't need to hire an expensive agency. You just need to follow these steps — and you'll start seeing results.

At DK Digital, we've helped 500+ Kenyan businesses implement this exact process. Let's get started.

🔍 Implementation Step 1: Get Found — 30 Minutes

This step takes about 30 minutes. It's the foundation of everything else — if Google can't find you, nothing else matters.

What to Do:

  • Set up Google Search Console (5 minutes) — Go to search.google.com/search-console and add your website. Verify ownership.
  • Submit your sitemap (2 minutes) — In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" and enter your sitemap URL (usually sitemap.xml).
  • Check your Coverage report (10 minutes) — Look for errors, fix any issues, and identify pages that aren't indexed.
  • Request indexing (10 minutes) — Use the URL Inspection tool to request indexing for your 5 most important pages.
  • Fix 404 errors (as needed) — Redirect broken pages to relevant working pages.

✅ Success Check:

After completing this step, your Coverage report should show "Valid" pages increasing. You should see "Crawled - currently not indexed" decreasing.

👀 Implementation Step 2: Get Noticed — 1-2 Hours

This step takes 1-2 hours. It's about making sure you show up when people search for what you offer.

What to Do:

  • Find your keywords (30 minutes) — Think about what your customers search for. Use Google's "People Also Ask" and autocomplete to find keywords. Focus on long-tail keywords.
  • Optimize your homepage title (5 minutes) — Include your primary keyword and make it compelling. Keep it under 60 characters.
  • Optimize your meta description (5 minutes) — Include keywords and a call-to-action. Keep it under 160 characters.
  • Check your H1 headings (10 minutes) — Make sure each page has one H1 that includes your primary keyword.
  • Optimize your top 3 pages (30 minutes) — For your 3 most important pages, add target keywords, update headings, and improve content.

📋 Quick Keyword Research Method:

  1. Type your main topic into Google
  2. Look at the "People Also Ask" section
  3. Look at the "Related Searches" at the bottom
  4. Write down 10-20 keyword ideas
  5. Pick the ones that are most relevant to your business

🤝 Implementation Step 3: Get Trusted — Ongoing

This step is ongoing. Building trust takes time, but every action you take builds momentum.

What to Do:

  • Create quality content (1-2 hours/week) — Publish blog posts, guides, or videos that help your customers. Focus on solving problems.
  • Ask for reviews (10 minutes/day) — Send a WhatsApp message to happy customers asking for a Google review. Make it easy with a direct link.
  • List on local directories (1-2 hours) — Add your business to Kenya Business Directory, Yellow Pages Kenya, and other local directories.
  • Build backlinks (ongoing) — Guest post on relevant blogs, partner with other businesses, and get listed as a resource.
  • Keep NAP consistent (check monthly) — Make sure your name, address, and phone number are the same everywhere online.

💡 Quick Win:

Write one blog post this week. Pick a question your customers frequently ask and answer it in detail. Share it on social media and email it to your list.

🛒 Implementation Step 4: Get Chosen — 1-2 Hours

This step takes 1-2 hours. It's about making sure visitors actually become customers.

What to Do:

  • Add clear CTAs (30 minutes) — Make sure every page has a clear next step. "Call Now," "Get a Free Quote," "Book Today."
  • Add WhatsApp button (10 minutes) — Add a click-to-WhatsApp button to your website. Many Kenyans prefer WhatsApp for inquiries.
  • Simplify forms (20 minutes) — Reduce form fields to only what you absolutely need. Name, email, phone, and message is enough.
  • Test mobile experience (15 minutes) — Open your website on your phone. Is it easy to read? Can you tap buttons easily? Fix any issues.
  • Add trust signals (15 minutes) — Add testimonials, reviews, and trust badges to your website.

✅ Quick Win:

Add your phone number and WhatsApp button to the header of your website. Make it visible on every page so customers can contact you easily.
