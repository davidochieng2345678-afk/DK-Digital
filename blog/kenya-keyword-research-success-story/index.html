Step 7: Create Content & Optimize – Turn Keywords into Rankings

How to write blog posts that rank in Kenya and how to optimize Shopify product pages for Kenya SEO is the final execution. We took our priority keywords and built pages around them.

📝 Content Creation Rules:

  • Shopify blog SEO Kenya – wrote 1,500+ word posts for each topic
  • Shopify product page SEO – inserted keywords in titles, meta descriptions, H1/H2s
  • Internal linking – linked blog posts to relevant product collections
  • Shopify image alt text Kenya – included keywords in image file names and alt text

📈 Optimization Checklist:

  • Meta title – keyword first, brand name last
  • Meta description – compelling + keyword + CTA
  • H1 – includes primary keyword
  • URL – short, descriptive, includes keyword
  • Content – at least 1,000 words for product pages, 1,500+ for blogs
  • Internal links – 3‑5 links to other relevant pages

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

We've seen many Kenyan businesses fail because they made these mistakes. Avoid them at all costs.

❌ Mistake 1: Ignoring Location Modifiers

Targeting global keywords like "fashion dresses" instead of "fashion dresses Nairobi." You'll never rank for global terms. Always add "Kenya," "Nairobi," "Mombasa," etc.

❌ Mistake 2: Not Matching Search Intent

Creating informational content for commercial keywords. If someone searches "buy dresses Nairobi," they want to buy, not read a blog. Match intent to content type.

❌ Mistake 3: Forgetting to Update Content

Publishing content and never updating it. Google rewards fresh content. Review and update your posts quarterly with new keywords and information.

❌ Mistake 4: Ignoring Technical SEO

Creating great content but having slow load times, broken links, or no schema. Technical SEO is the foundation. Fix it first.

❌ Mistake 5: Not Tracking Performance

You can't improve what you don't measure. Set up Google Search Console and Analytics from day one. Track rankings, traffic, and conversions.

❌ Mistake 6: Ignoring Mobile Users

Most Kenyans use mobile to search. Ensure your site is mobile-friendly, fast, and easy to navigate on small screens.

Frequently Asked Questions – Kenya Keyword Research

We've compiled the most common questions from Kenyan business owners about keyword research, SEO, and growing organic traffic. Here are the answers.

12 questions Expert answers Kenya-focused
1

How long does keyword research take?

The initial research takes 1-2 weeks. But keyword research is ongoing – review your list monthly and do a full audit quarterly. In our case study, the initial research took 10 days, but we continued refining over 6 months.

2

What's the best tool for keyword research in Kenya?

Google Keyword Planner with the Kenya location filter is the most accurate. Ubersuggest (3 free searches/day) and AnswerThePublic are excellent for expansion. For competitor analysis, use Ahrefs or SEMrush.

3

How many keywords should I target?

Start with 50-100 keywords across your product categories. In our case study, we found 200+ keywords – but we started with the top 50 and expanded from there. Quality matters more than quantity.

4

Should I target informational or transactional keywords?

Both. Transactional keywords drive sales. Informational keywords build authority. Use the 80/20 rule – 80% informational content, 20% promotional content.

5

What if my business is in a small town like Kisumu or Eldoret?

That's a big advantage – less competition! Use location modifiers like "Kisumu," "Eldoret," or "Western Kenya." You'll rank faster and capture highly targeted local traffic.

6

How do I know if a keyword is worth targeting?

Use three criteria: 1) Search volume 50-500/month in Kenya, 2) Keyword Difficulty under 30, 3) Commercial or transactional intent. Keywords with all three are "low-hanging fruit."

7

How often should I update my keyword strategy?

Review your keyword performance monthly and do a full strategy update quarterly. The Kenyan market changes – new trends emerge, competitors enter, and customer behaviour shifts.

8

Can I do keyword research myself, or do I need to hire an agency?

You can absolutely do it yourself – this guide gives you everything you need. However, it requires time and consistency. If you have the budget and want faster results, hiring an experienced Kenyan SEO agency can accelerate the process.

9

What's the single most important thing for Kenyan SEO?

Location-specific, long-tail, BOFU keywords. Use "Nairobi," "Kenya," or your specific town in every keyword. Target "buy," "price," "shop," "best" – these signal commercial intent. That's the secret sauce.

10

How do I track keyword rankings in Kenya?

Use Google Search Console – it shows your average position, impressions, and clicks for each keyword. For more detailed tracking, tools like Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Ubersuggest give daily rank updates. Always set location to "Kenya."

11

What's the difference between BOFU, MOFU, and TOFU keywords?

TOFU (Top of Funnel) – informational (e.g., "what is Ankara fabric?"). MOFU (Middle) – comparative (e.g., "Ankara vs Kitenge"). BOFU (Bottom) – transactional (e.g., "buy Ankara dress Nairobi"). Focus on BOFU for sales.

12

Do I need a blog to rank, or can I just use product pages?

You need both. Product pages capture commercial intent. Blog posts capture informational intent. The blog builds authority, drives traffic, and feeds internal links to your product pages. In our strategy, we created 8 blog posts alongside 12 product pages – they work together.
