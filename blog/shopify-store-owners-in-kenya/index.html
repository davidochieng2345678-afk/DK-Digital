6 Proven Shopify SEO Strategies

These six strategies have helped 50+ Shopify stores in Kenya boost organic traffic, reduce ad spend, and increase sales.

1

Optimize Product Pages for Long‑Tail Local Keywords

Product pages are your most important SEO assets. Each product page should be optimized with relevant long-tail keywords that include location and buyer intent.

🔧 Action Items:

  • Include keywords in product titles, meta descriptions, and headings
  • Write 300+ word descriptions highlighting features, benefits, and use cases
  • Use location keywords like "Kenya", "Nairobi", or "Mombasa"
  • Add buyer-intent words like "buy", "price", "shop", "delivery"

📊 Example Keywords:

  • "affordable yoga pants Kenya"
  • "best wireless chargers Nairobi"
  • "organic coffee beans delivered Nairobi"

Pro Tip: Use tools like Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, or SEMrush to find long-tail keywords with 100-500 monthly searches.

2

Improve Site Speed & Mobile Experience

Most Kenyan online shoppers use mobile devices. Google considers mobile experience and speed as critical ranking factors. A fast, mobile-friendly store converts better and ranks higher.

🔧 Action Items:

  • Compress images – use TinyIMG or Crush.pics
  • Remove unused apps – each app adds load time
  • Enable caching – Shopify's built-in caching
  • Use responsive themes – mobile-first design

⚡ Speed Targets:

  • Current: 5-6 seconds
  • Target: Under 3 seconds
  • Result: 20-30% increase in conversions

Mobile-first indexing: Google uses your mobile site to determine rankings. If your store isn't mobile-friendly, you're losing customers.

Use Structured Data for Shopify Products

Implement Schema markup for products, reviews, and FAQ. Structured data helps Google understand your pages and may enhance listings in search results with rich snippets (price, availability, reviews).

🔧 Action Items:

  • Add Review Schema (star ratings, testimonials)
