The 4 Pillars of the DK Digital SEO Audit

Most SEO audits only scratch the surface. They check a few technical issues and call it done. But true growth requires a deep dive into every aspect of your SEO — from the technical foundation to the content you create to the backlinks you build.

⚙️ Pillar 1: Technical SEO Audit

Technical SEO is the foundation of everything. If Google can't properly crawl, index, or understand your site, nothing else matters.

We analyse every technical aspect of your website, including:

🚀

Site Speed & Performance

Slow sites lose rankings and customers. We test your site's speed on desktop and mobile — and identify exactly what's slowing you down.

We check:

  • Core Web Vitals (LCP, FID, CLS)

  • Caching and CDN usage

Mobile Responsiveness

With 63% of searches on mobile, your site must work perfectly on phones. We test every aspect of your mobile experience.

We check:

  • Readable text on mobile
  • Tap targets (buttons, links)
🕷️

Crawlability & Indexing

Google needs to find and understand your pages. If it can't, you won't rank. We identify every issue that blocks crawlers.

We check:

  • Robots.txt configuration
  • XML sitemap status
  • Indexing issues and noindex tags
  • Canonical tags
  • Redirect chains (301, 302)
🔗

Internal Linking & Site Structure

A strong internal linking structure helps Google understand your content hierarchy and distributes authority across your pages.

We check:

  • Internal linking structure
  • Broken links (404 errors)

  • Breadcrumb implementation

Technical SEO Audit

⚙️ The Foundation: Ensuring Google can properly crawl, index, and understand your website

Technical SEO is the backbone of your entire SEO strategy. If your website has technical issues, nothing else matters — Google won't be able to find, crawl, or rank your pages.

Most Kenyan businesses have significant technical issues that they're completely unaware of. Slow loading speeds, mobile usability problems, broken links, and crawl errors are silently killing their rankings and driving customers away.

At DK Digital, we've conducted 100+ technical SEO audits for Kenyan businesses. In this section, I'll show you exactly what we check and how to fix the most common issues.

🚀 Key Area 1: Site Speed & Performance

Speed is a direct ranking factor. Google has confirmed that page speed affects rankings — and users expect pages to load in under 3 seconds.

📊 The Data:

  • 53% of mobile users abandon sites that take over 3 seconds to load
  • Every 1-second delay reduces conversions by 7%
  • 2-second load time is the average for top-ranking pages
  • 80% of Kenyan websites load in over 4 seconds

What We Check:

  • Page load time: We test your site on desktop and mobile using Google PageSpeed Insights. Target: under 3 seconds.
  • Core Web Vitals: We analyse Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), First Input Delay (FID), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
  • Image optimization: We check image sizes, formats, and compression. Unoptimized images are the #1 cause of slow load times.
  • Server response time: We test your hosting provider's performance. Slow hosting = slow site.
  • Caching: We check if browser caching is enabled to speed up repeat visits.
  • CDN usage: We check if you're using a Content Delivery Network to serve content faster.

📝 Speed Score Benchmarks:

Score Range Rating Action
90-100 Excellent Maintain
70-89 Good Optimize further
40-69 Poor Priority fix
0-39 Critical Emergency fix

📱 Key Area 2: Mobile Responsiveness

Google uses mobile-first indexing. This means Google predominantly uses the mobile version of your site for ranking and indexing. If your mobile site is broken, your rankings will suffer.

📊 The Data:

  • 63% of Google searches in Kenya happen on mobile
  • 60% of Kenyan websites have mobile usability issues
  • 82% of mobile users use "near me" searches to find businesses
  • Mobile sites that are hard to use have 5x higher bounce rates

What We Check:

  • Google Mobile-Friendly Test: We run your site through Google's official tool to check compliance.
  • Mobile usability issues: We check for common problems like text too small, clickable elements too close, and content wider than screen.
  • Responsive design: We test how your site adapts to different screen sizes and devices.
  • Click-to-call functionality: We check if phone numbers are clickable on mobile (tel: links).
  • Mobile navigation: We test if your mobile menu is easy to use and intuitive.
  • Mobile forms: We check if forms are easy to fill out on mobile (correct input types, auto-fill, etc.).

💡 Quick Mobile Test:

Open your website on your phone right now. Can you read everything without zooming? Are buttons easy to tap? Can you find your phone number and call it with one tap? If not, you have mobile issues to fix.

🕷️ Key Area 3: Crawlability & Indexing

If Google can't crawl your site, you can't rank. We check every aspect of your site's crawlability and indexing status.

What We Check:

  • Robots.txt: We check if your robots.txt file is blocking important pages or resources.
  • XML sitemap: We verify that your sitemap exists, is up-to-date, and includes all important pages.
  • Indexing status: We check how many of your pages are indexed in Google (Search Console coverage report).
  • Noindex tags: We identify any pages accidentally set to noindex.
  • Canonical tags: We check if canonical tags are correctly implemented to avoid duplicate content.
  • Redirect chains: We find any 301/302 redirect chains that slow down crawling and waste link equity.
  • 404 errors: We identify broken pages and recommend fixes (redirects or restoration).
  • Crawl depth: We check how many clicks it takes to reach important pages from the homepage (target: 3 clicks or fewer).

📋 Common Crawlability Issues:

  • Missing sitemap — 40% of sites don't have an XML sitemap
  • Unindexed pages — 30% of pages aren't indexed by Google
  • Redirect chains — 25% of sites have unnecessary redirects
  • Broken links — Average site has 15+ broken links (404s)
  • Orphan pages — 20% of pages have no internal links pointing to them

🔗 Key Area 4: Internal Linking & Site Structure

Internal linking is one of the most underrated SEO tactics. A strong internal linking structure helps Google understand your content hierarchy and distributes authority across your pages.

What We Check:

  • Internal linking structure: We analyse how your pages link to each other and identify any gaps.
  • Link authority flow: We check that link equity is flowing to your most important pages.
  • Anchor text: We review the anchor text used in internal links — it should be descriptive and keyword-rich.
  • Orphan pages: We identify pages with no internal links pointing to them and recommend fixes.
  • Breadcrumbs: We check if breadcrumbs are implemented and properly structured.
  • URL structure: We analyse your URL hierarchy and recommend improvements for SEO and user experience.

✅ Best Practice:

  • Every page should be accessible within 3 clicks from the homepage
  • Each page should have at least 3-5 internal links pointing to it
  • Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here")
  • Link from high-authority pages to pages that need a boost
  • Implement breadcrumb navigation for user experience and SEO

🔒 Key Area 5: Security & HTTPS

HTTPS is a ranking signal. Google has confirmed that secure sites get a slight ranking boost. More importantly, users trust HTTPS sites — and trust is essential for conversions.

What We Check:

  • SSL certificate: We check if your site has a valid SSL certificate and is served over HTTPS.
  • Mixed content: We check for insecure content (HTTP) loading on HTTPS pages — this can break security and hurt trust.
  • Redirects: We check that all HTTP URLs redirect to HTTPS versions (301 redirect).
  • HSTS: We check if HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) is enabled to enforce HTTPS connections.

🚨 Warning:

Google Chrome now marks HTTP sites as "Not Secure" in the address bar. This scares customers away and reduces conversions. If your site isn't on HTTPS, fix this immediately.

On-Page SEO Audit

📝 The Content Foundation: Ensuring every page on your site is optimized to rank and convert

On-page SEO is how you tell Google what your pages are about. If your content isn't optimized, Google won't know to rank you for relevant searches — and users won't find what they're looking for.

Most Kenyan businesses have significant on-page issues. Missing title tags, thin content, duplicate pages, and poor keyword targeting are silently killing their rankings and confusing both Google and users.

At DK Digital, we've analysed 100+ Kenyan websites for on-page SEO issues. In this section, I'll show you exactly what we check and how to fix the most common problems.

📋 Key Area 1: Meta Tags & Headings

Your title tags and meta descriptions are the first thing people see in search results. They must be optimized for both rankings and click-throughs.

📊 The Data:

  • 70% of Kenyan websites have poorly optimized title tags
  • 50% of pages lack meta descriptions entirely
  • Optimized title tags can increase CTR by 30-50%
  • Pages with keyword-rich H1s rank significantly higher

What We Check:

  • Title tags: We check length (50-60 characters), keyword placement (primary keyword near the front), uniqueness (no duplicate titles), and click-through appeal.
  • Meta descriptions: We check length (150-160 characters), keyword inclusion, and compelling copy that drives clicks.
  • H1 headings: We check that every page has exactly one H1 tag with the primary keyword and clear messaging.
  • H2/H3 headings: We check that subheadings are used to structure content and include secondary keywords.
  • Missing or duplicate tags: We identify pages with missing or duplicate meta tags and recommend fixes.

📝 Title Tag Best Practices:

❌ Bad Title Tag:

"Home | Our Company | Nairobi"

Weak, generic, no keywords

✅ Good Title Tag:

"Best Coffee Shop in Kilimani | Java House Nairobi"

Keyword-rich, location, brand

📖 Key Area 2: Content Quality & Optimization

Content is still king. But it needs to be high-quality, comprehensive, and optimized for both users and search engines.

What We Check:

  • Content length: We check if your key pages have sufficient depth (target: 1,500+ words for pillar pages, 500-800 for supporting pages).
  • Keyword usage: We check that primary and secondary keywords are used naturally throughout the content (not stuffed).
  • LSI and related keywords: We check for semantic keywords that help Google understand your content's context.
  • Readability: We check if your content is easy to read (short sentences, clear structure, plain language).
  • Formatting: We check for proper use of headings, bullet points, bold text, and other formatting that improves readability.
  • Duplicate content: We identify pages with duplicate or near- duplicate content and recommend consolidation or canonicalization.
  • Content freshness: We check if your content is up-to-date (recently updated or reviewed).

📋 Content Length Benchmarks:

Page Type Recommended Length Why
Pillar Pages 2,000+ words Comprehensive authority content
Blog Posts 1,000-2,000 words In-depth but scannable
Service Pages 800-1,500 words Detailed but focused
Product Pages 300-800 words Concise but complete

💡 Common Content Issues We Find:

  • Thin content: 60% of Kenyan pages have less than 300 words
  • Duplicate content: 35% of sites have duplicate page content
  • No keyword targeting: 50% of pages don't target any specific keywords
  • Poor readability: 40% of content is too complex for the average reader
  • Outdated content: 45% of pages haven't been updated in over a year

🖼️ Key Area 3: Image & Media Optimization

Images can boost rankings — or kill them. Properly optimized images improve page speed, user experience, and accessibility. Unoptimized images do the opposite.

What We Check:

  • Image file sizes: We check if images are compressed for web use (target: under 100KB for most images).
  • Alt text: We check that all images have descriptive alt text (including keywords where relevant) for accessibility and SEO.
  • Image file names: We check that image filenames are descriptive (e.g., "coffee-shop-kilimani.jpg" instead of "IMG_1234.jpg").
  • Lazy loading: We check if lazy loading is implemented to load images only when they're visible.

Common SEO Issues We Find in Kenyan Audits

🚨 The Reality: 94% of websites have critical SEO issues. Here are the most common ones we uncover.

We've conducted 100+ comprehensive SEO audits for Kenyan businesses. And we've seen the same issues over and over again — issues that are silently killing rankings and costing businesses traffic, leads, and revenue.

The good news? Every single one of these issues is fixable. Once you know what's wrong, you can fix it and start seeing results.

In this section, I'll share the most common issues we find in our audits — and exactly how to fix them.

🐢 Issue #1: Slow Page Speed

Found in: 80% of audits

The problem: Most Kenyan websites are too slow. Pages take 4+ seconds to load — and every second of delay costs you 7% of conversions.

🔍 Why It Happens:

  • Unoptimized images — Large, uncompressed images are the #1 speed killer
  • Cheap hosting — Shared hosting with limited resources
  • No caching — Browser caching not enabled
  • Heavy themes and plugins — Bloated code slowing everything down
  • No CDN — Content Delivery Network not used

✅ How to Fix It:

  • Compress all images using TinyPNG or Squoosh
  • Convert images to WebP format
  • Enable browser caching
  • Upgrade to better hosting (VPS or dedicated)
  • Use a CDN like Cloudflare
  • Remove unused plugins and themes

📱 Issue #2: Poor Mobile Experience

Found in: 60% of audits

The problem: With 63% of searches on mobile, your site must work perfectly on phones. Most Kenyan sites fail this test.

🔍 Why It Happens:

  • Desktop-only design — Not responsive
  • Text too small — Requires zooming to read
  • Buttons too small — Hard to tap on mobile
  • Content wider than screen — Horizontal scrolling required
  • No click-to-call — Phone numbers not tappable

✅ How to Fix It:

  • Implement responsive design
  • Use minimum 16px font size for body text
  • Make buttons at least 44px tall
  • Test with Google's Mobile-Friendly Test
  • Add click-to-call links (tel:)

📋 Issue #3: Missing or Poor Meta Tags

Found in: 70% of audits

The problem: Your title tags and meta descriptions are the first thing people see in search results. Missing or weak tags mean low click-through rates and poor rankings.

🔍 Why It Happens:

  • No meta descriptions — Google auto-generates (poorly)
  • No keywords in titles — Missing primary keywords

✅ How to Fix It:

  • Add unique title tags (50-60 characters) to every page
  • Include primary keywords at the start of titles
  • Add compelling meta descriptions (150-160 characters)
  • Use action-oriented language to drive clicks
  • Use a plugin like Yoast to manage meta tags

📖 Issue #4: Thin or Duplicate Content

Found in: 60% of audits

The problem: Google needs substantial, unique content to rank your pages. Thin or duplicate content confuses Google and hurts your rankings.

🔍 Why It Happens:

✅ How to Fix It:

  • Regularly update and refresh old content
